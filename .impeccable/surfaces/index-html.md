# Surface brief: MathQuest app (index.html)

Scope: the whole app shell (accueil par niveau, chapitre, Entraînement, Examen, Duel 2 joueurs, Battle 2-4 joueurs, résultats, progrès). Mode: Operate. Audience: collégiens 6e-3e (priorité 6e-5e) sur téléphone d'abord, tablette et ordinateur ensuite.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each level is a sneaker colorway and every game launches like a drop. Refuses the dark gamified dashboard with emoji tiles and glow, and the white-card learning-app default.

OWN-WORLD: Sail ground #F4F3EF, ink #111, one saturated colorway per level owning whole regions (6e volt #D4FF3A, 5e university blue, 4e grape, 3e infrared). Archivo variable: condensed 900 uppercase display, normal width for UI, tabular figures everywhere. Black pills, 2px ink rules, square-ish size-run cells, one 8px module.

STORY: The student sees their level colorway, resumes in one tap, reads mastery per notion as filled cells, and challenges a friend from the "À deux" tab.

FIRST VIEWPORT: top bar wordmark + level/streak pills; 4-level segmented control; full-bleed colorway block with a giant cropped level numeral, recommended chapter name in condensed caps and a black "Lancer la série" pill bottom-left; then "Notions de 6e" list with 2px ink rules and 5 mastery cells per row; bottom tab bar Jouer / À deux / Progrès in ink.
